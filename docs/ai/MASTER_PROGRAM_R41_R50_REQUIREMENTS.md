# Master Requirements — R41 through R50

Canonical copy of the Dropbox prompt-library product objective for the operator productionization program.

Product objective: turn ChakraOps into a reliable daily manual advisory workstation for CSP, CC, Shares, Manage, Exit, Watch/Near Miss, or Stay in Cash.

Cross-program requirements:
- one authoritative daily workflow
- no decorative/static financial data in LIVE mode
- every route/card/control has a purpose
- recommendation explainability includes measurement, threshold, unit, source, timestamp and portfolio impact
- expected mutations persist across reload/restart
- screen reads do not trigger unnecessary full-universe/provider work
- critical errors surface truthfully; no fabricated defaults
- every meaningful control is browser-tested
- lint/tests/types/build/security become blocking
- every release produces reproducible evidence
- final R50 produces redacted evidence ZIP

Trade-quality rule:
Do not optimize for trade count. Verify zero opportunities are not caused by broken wiring. Do not approve because risk data is missing. Shares must not be incorrectly coupled to CSP-only requirements. CSP/CC selection must use correct DTE, delta, liquidity and collateral. Aggressive profile may only relax opportunity selection with documented compensating risk controls. No evidence-free threshold retune.

Target daily path:
Start → health → explicit evaluation/review → top 5–7 actions or Stay in Cash → candidate detail → CSP vs Shares/manage Wheel → manual ticket → operator executes in broker → record actual fill → monitor/manage → review alerts/system only as needed.

Known external gap:
ORATS `/hist/options` may remain unentitled. Do not fake historical-options completeness.
