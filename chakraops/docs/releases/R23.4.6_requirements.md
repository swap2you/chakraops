# R23.4.6 — Symbol Diagnostics UX + Explainability Pack

**Goal:** Make Symbol Diagnostics (Execution Console) feel premium and less cluttered via progressive disclosure. Copilot as slide-over drawer (default closed). Reduce "—" spam; show "Not available" with short why when missing due to data.

---

## Acceptance criteria

### A) SymbolDiagnosticsPage restructure
- **Hero header:** symbol, price, quote_as_of, verdict, score, band, regime badge, primary reason(s). Recompute and Copilot button in header.
- **Options tab:** 3 accordions (collapsed by default except first):
  1. **Trade:** Candidates table, Exit Plan, Targets & hold-time
  2. **Technicals:** Technical details, Multi-timeframe levels
  3. **Risk & Details:** Risk flags, Data freshness, collapsible Details (as-of/inputs, debug)
- Layout balanced, full width on large screens (no permanent narrow right column).

### B) Copilot UI
- Replace permanent right-side panel with "Copilot" button in header (or floating).
- Click opens right-side drawer overlay containing CopilotPanel.
- Drawer: close (X), ESC key; on close, main content uses full width.

### C) Explainability
- Tooltips for: RSI, ATR, ATR%, Regime, Support, Resistance, Delta band, Bar count, Hold-time estimate, Targets basis, Invalidation.
- Targets & hold-time card copy: "Targets from next resistances above spot (passed levels skipped). Hold-time is ATR-distance based and rough (not a promise)."
- When targets_already_exceeded: badge + short guidance ("Targets already exceeded at snapshot; recompute for updated levels.").

### D) Reduce dashed fields
- Non-applicable: hide (do not show "—").
- Missing due to absent diagnostics: show "Not available (no diagnostics snapshot)" where appropriate; keep rest of UI stable.

---

## Non-negotiables
- No new prose persisted in decision_latest.json.
- No raw FAIL_/WARN_ in UI.
- Copilot remains read-only and optional.
- No new trading/actions endpoints.
